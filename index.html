📖 What is Forex?

Foreign exchange (Forex) is the global marketplace for exchanging national currencies. It's the largest financial market with $7.5 trillion daily volume.

The Forex market operates 24 hours a day, 5 days a week across major financial centers: Sydney, Tokyo, London, and New York. Currencies are always traded in pairs, and profits are made from exchange rate fluctuations.

📈 Technical Analysis Basics

Technical analysis involves studying price charts and patterns to predict future movements.

Key concepts:
• Support & Resistance
• Trend Lines
• Moving Averages (SMA, EMA)
• RSI (Relative Strength Index)
• MACD (Moving Average Convergence Divergence)
• Candlestick Patterns

🛡️ Risk Management

Never risk more than 1-2% of your account on a single trade. Use stop-loss orders always.

Golden rules:
• Risk/Reward ratio should be at least 1:2
• Keep a trading journal
• Don't revenge trade after losses
• Diversify across currency pairs
• Set daily loss limits

💡 Fundamental Analysis

Understanding economic indicators that move currencies: interest rates, GDP, inflation, employment data.

Key indicators to watch:
• Central Bank interest rate decisions
• Non-Farm Payrolls (NFP)
• Consumer Price Index (CPI)
• GDP growth rates
• Political events and elections
